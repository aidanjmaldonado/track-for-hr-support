# Track ECG Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 1, 2026  
**Developer:** Aidan Maldonado  
**App:** Track ECG for Polar H10

## Overview

Track ECG is an independent iOS application for connecting to a Polar H10 sensor, viewing sensor measurements, saving recordings, and exporting recordings at your direction.

## Information handled by the app

When you connect a Polar H10, Track ECG processes the sensor measurements needed for its functionality, which can include ECG, heart rate, beat-to-beat intervals, accelerometer samples, device connection information, and recording metadata. Recordings may include timestamps. The app also stores user-selected preferences such as appearance and language settings.

## Storage and developer access

The reviewed implementation stores saved recordings locally on your iPhone. It does not implement developer-operated accounts, analytics collection, advertising tracking, or automatic uploading of recordings to a developer-operated server. The developer does not receive your locally stored sensor recordings merely because you use the app.

## Bluetooth access

Track ECG uses Bluetooth access to discover and connect to compatible Polar H10 sensors and receive their measurements. Bluetooth permission is required for the sensor connection features.

## Exporting and sharing

You decide whether to export or share a recording. If you select another app, a cloud storage provider, email, or any other destination using the iOS sharing interface, the data you share becomes subject to the selected destination's privacy practices. Review the destination's policies before sharing sensitive measurements.

## Retention and deletion

Saved recordings remain in the app's local storage until you delete them through the app or remove the app and its data, subject to device backups and iOS behavior. Exported copies remain wherever you chose to save or share them and must be managed at those destinations separately. Removing the app may permanently erase recordings stored only on the device.

## Third-party software and platform services

Track ECG uses the Polar BLE SDK to communicate with supported sensors and relies on Apple's operating-system services for Bluetooth, local storage, and sharing. Apple and third-party destinations may process information under their own policies. The developer should recheck this policy whenever the app's dependencies or data flows change.

## Medical use

Track ECG records and displays measurements; it is not intended to provide a medical diagnosis or replace professional medical care.

## Policy updates and questions

The policy may be updated when Track ECG's functionality or data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. For general questions, visit the [support repository](https://github.com/aidanjmaldonado/trackecg-support). **Do not post private health data in a public GitHub issue.** For privacy-sensitive requests, contact the developer through a private contact method provided on the developer's [GitHub profile](https://github.com/aidanjmaldonado), if available.
