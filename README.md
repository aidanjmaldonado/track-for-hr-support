# Track for HR Support

Track for HR is an iOS app for recording and exporting data from a Polar H10 chest strap. The app records heart rate, RR intervals, and accelerometer data. Recordings are stored locally on the iPhone.

The optional Workouts purchase adds activity tracking, GPS routes for outdoor workouts, intervals, and Apple Health integration.

Track for HR is an independent app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Polar Electro Oy.

## Reporting a problem

Include the iPhone model, iOS version, Track for HR version, and steps to reproduce the problem. For recording and export problems, include the export format and relevant recording settings.

Do not post recordings, location history, medical information, or other personal data in a public issue.
